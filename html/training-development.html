Empowering Construction Professionals Through Continuous CPD Learning

UBBIM is the technology developer behind ACPM Academy — a Continuing Professional Development (CPD) learning and development platform whitelabeled for the Association of Construction Project Managers Malaysia (ACPM). Built for the continuous training and development of ACPM members and construction professionals, our platform helps learners advance their careers through structured professional education.
