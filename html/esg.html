Environmental, Social & Governance

Building a Better Tomorrow

At UBBIM, sustainability is not a buzzword — it is embedded in how we operate, how we give back, and how we govern.

We believe that businesses have a responsibility beyond profit. Our ESG framework drives every decision we make — from protecting Malaysia's coastlines to uplifting underserved communities and upholding the highest standards of corporate integrity.

Environment

Mangrove Replanting

Malaysia's mangrove forests are natural guardians of our coastlines — protecting against erosion, filtering pollutants, and serving as critical nurseries for marine life. UBBIM actively participates in mangrove replanting initiatives to restore these vital ecosystems.

Coastal Protection

Replanting mangroves reinforces natural coastal barriers, reducing the impact of storms and erosion on vulnerable shorelines.

Carbon Sequestration

Mangroves absorb up to 5x more carbon than terrestrial forests, making them one of nature's most effective climate solutions.

Biodiversity Haven

Restored mangroves create thriving habitats for fish, crustaceans, and bird species essential to Malaysia's marine ecology.

Social

Community Care

We are committed to uplifting the communities around us. Through structured social programmes, UBBIM delivers direct, consistent support to families and children who need it most.

Monthly Food Rations

Every month, we distribute essential food supplies to underprivileged families, ensuring no one in our community goes hungry.

School Uniforms for Kids

We provide school uniforms and basic educational supplies to children from low-income households, removing barriers to education.

Governance

Integrity by Design

Good governance is the foundation of trust. UBBIM operates under a strict anti-bribery framework certified to international standards, ensuring transparency, fairness, and accountability in every transaction and partnership.

Anti-Bribery ISO Certification

ISO 37001

UBBIM is certified under the ISO 37001 Anti-Bribery Management System. This means our policies, procedures, and controls are independently verified to prevent, detect, and address bribery across all levels of our organisation.

Whistleblower Protection

Safe Reporting

We maintain a confidential whistleblower channel where employees, partners, and stakeholders can report concerns without fear of retaliation. Every report is investigated thoroughly and impartially.

Global Alignment

Aligned with SDG 17

UBBIM's ESG strategy is anchored in the UN Sustainable Development Goals, with a special focus on SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals. We believe lasting change happens when organisations, communities, and governments work together.

Multi-Stakeholder Partnerships

We collaborate with environmental NGOs, local councils, schools, and industry bodies to amplify the impact of our programmes.

Knowledge Sharing

We openly share our ESG learnings with clients, peers, and the wider construction industry to accelerate sustainable practices across the sector.

Measurable Impact

Every initiative is tracked, measured, and reported. We hold ourselves accountable to the communities we serve and the planet we share.

Our Commitment to the UN SDGs

UBBIM actively contributes to SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 13 (Climate Action), SDG 14 (Life Below Water), and SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals).
